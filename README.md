### Coach Works

Job management for coach builders: food trucks, hearse vans, mobile ATMs, car customisation and side wheel fitting. It is built on ERPNext 16.

**How a job flows**

1. **New Job**: pick the customer and the vehicle, and describe the work.
2. **Create → Quotation**: make as many revised quotes as needed. Submitting the first one moves the job to *Quoted*.
3. **Accept for Job** (on the quotation the customer agreed to): its items become the job's charges and the job moves to *Approved*.
4. **Move to Build → Paint / Finishing → Ready → Delivered**: one button press per step, and every move is recorded in the Stage History.
5. **Create → Sales Invoice**, then **Create → Payment**: both use ERPNext's own GST-ready forms.

Everything is under one **Coach Works** sidebar:
- Dashboard
- Jobs
- Quotations
- Vehicles
- Customers
- Invoices
- Payments
- Reports
- Setup

### Installation

```bash
bench get-app https://github.com/tbocloud/coach_works --branch main
bench --site <site> install-app coach_works
```

Install sets up the work stages, vehicle types and the company in **Coach Work Settings**.

### Development

- The sidebar, dock and app tile are generated by `python3 scripts/gen_desk.py`.
- Formatting is handled by `pre-commit` (ruff, prettier, eslint).
